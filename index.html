Small apps
for a new world.

“A new world is struggling to be born,” as a pretty swell guy once said. I believe that this new world will be different, and, hopefully, a little bit better.

The apps, tools, and services on this page are my contribution to the new world. A freer world: without gatekeepers, without any of the usual nonsense that we are so used to these days.
